    How Mounjaro Treatment Works in Burbank, CA

    How Mounjaro Works in Burbank

    Tirzepatide activates two receptors at once. Through the GIP and GLP-1 receptors, it prompts insulin release when glucose is high, lowers glucagon and keeps food in the stomach longer. Many people eat less without trying. The same molecule sold as Zepbound carries the weight management label.

    Who Mounjaro Is For

    Mounjaro is approved for adults with type 2 diabetes. The label covers improving glucose control in type 2 diabetes alongside diet and activity, and it excludes type 1 diabetes and weight loss alone. If you do not have diabetes, your provider will likely suggest Zepbound instead. Off-label use is disclosed when it happens.

    Mounjaro Dosing Schedule in Burbank

    The first four weeks are at 2.5 mg. That dose is a starter meant to help your body adjust, and it is not expected to control blood sugar on its own. The dose then goes to 5 mg and can rise in 2.5 mg steps every four weeks to 7.5, 10, 12.5 and a maximum of 15 mg. Your glucose response decides each step.

    What to Expect Week by Week

    Early weeks bring smaller portions and fewer snacks. Nausea or loose stools are most likely in the day or two after starting and after each increase, and they usually settle within a week as your body adjusts. Blood sugar readings often improve within the first month or two. The A1C drawn around month three shows the real trend, and the SURPASS clinical trials reported average reductions near 2 points at higher doses.

    Mounjaro Side Effects in Burbank

    Nausea is the most common complaint. Diarrhea, decreased appetite, vomiting, constipation, indigestion and stomach pain also show up, mostly in the early weeks and after each dose increase. Less common serious risks include pancreatitis, gallbladder problems, kidney injury from dehydration and worsening diabetic retinopathy. Mounjaro has a boxed warning for thyroid C-cell tumors seen in rodents.

    Eligibility and Screening

    Your provider looks first at your type 2 diabetes diagnosis and a recent A1C. They then review your medications, weight, kidney function history and family history of thyroid cancer. People with a personal or family history of medullary thyroid carcinoma or MEN 2 should not use Mounjaro. Pregnancy does too.

    Mounjaro Compared With Related Options in Burbank

    Same medicine, two labels. Mounjaro is approved for type 2 diabetes and Zepbound for weight management, and that difference affects insurance coverage and how your provider writes the prescription. Ozempic, by contrast, is semaglutide and works through the GLP-1 receptor only. Compounded tirzepatide is not FDA-approved and is a different product from Mounjaro.
