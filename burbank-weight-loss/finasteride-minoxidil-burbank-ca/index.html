    How Finasteride + Minoxidil Works in Burbank, CA

    How Finasteride + Minoxidil Works in Burbank

    Finasteride blocks type 2 5-alpha reductase, the enzyme that turns testosterone into DHT. With less DHT around, the follicles on the crown and temples stop shrinking as quickly, and many hold their size for years instead of fading out. Minoxidil works on a separate track. It keeps hairs in their growing phase longer, so they come in thicker.

    Who This Combination Is For

    It is mainly for adult men with male pattern hair loss, such as a receding hairline or thinning crown. It works best when started early, while follicles are still producing hair, because areas that have been smooth for years rarely come back with any medicine. Some women past menopause use it off-label. Women who could become pregnant cannot.

    Dosing and Daily Schedule in Burbank

    Oral finasteride is FDA-approved at 1 mg once a day for male pattern hair loss. Topical minoxidil is applied to a dry scalp once or twice daily, while low-dose oral minoxidil, often 1.25 to 2.5 mg, is an off-label daily pill some men prefer to avoid the mess. One spray daily also works. The combination spray is applied to the scalp each day.

    What to Expect Month by Month

    Expect some shedding in months one and two. The early shed is common and usually temporary. By months three to six, most men see less loss and some thickening at the crown. Long-term trials showed most men held their hair for years. Give it a year.

    Side Effects of Finasteride + Minoxidil in Burbank

    Finasteride may lower libido, affect erections or reduce ejaculate volume in about one to two percent of men. These usually resolve after stopping, and rare mood changes, including low mood, should be reported to your provider right away. Scalp dryness is the usual topical issue. Oral minoxidil can cause body hair, ankle swelling or a faster heartbeat. The spray may reduce systemic side effects.

    Eligibility and Screening

    Your provider asks about liver health, heart conditions, blood pressure, mood history and sexual health. Expect questions about your medication list and family plans, because both can shape the choice. PSA results drop by about half. Women who could become pregnant should not handle crushed or broken tablets.

    How It Compares to Other Options in Burbank

    Finasteride + minoxidil is the standard first combination for men. When finasteride falls short, dutasteride is the usual next step, off-label for hair. Minoxidil + GHK-Cu is topical-only. Minoxidil alone helps, but it does nothing about DHT.
