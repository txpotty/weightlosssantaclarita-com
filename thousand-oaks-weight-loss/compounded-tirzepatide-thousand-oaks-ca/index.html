    What Compounded Tirzepatide Is in Thousand Oaks

    Compounded tirzepatide is prepared by a licensed compounding pharmacy for an individual patient rather than mass produced. The active ingredient matches the brand-name drugs.     Side Effects of Compounded Tirzepatide in Thousand Oaks

    Nausea, diarrhea, constipation and vomiting are the most common side effects, and they usually ease within a few weeks. Drawing too many units is a known cause of severe vomiting. Tirzepatide carries a boxed warning about thyroid C-cell tumors seen in rodents.
