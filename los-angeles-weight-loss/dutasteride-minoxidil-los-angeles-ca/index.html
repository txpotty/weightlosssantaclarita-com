    How Dutasteride + Minoxidil Works in Los Angeles, CA

    How Dutasteride + Minoxidil Works in Los Angeles

    Dutasteride blocks both type 1 and type 2 5-alpha reductase, the enzymes that turn testosterone into DHT. Blocking both enzymes cuts DHT further than finasteride can. Less DHT, less shrinking. Minoxidil adds a separate benefit by lengthening the growth phase. The pair covers cause and growth.

    Who This Combination Is For

    It is mainly for adult men with male pattern hair loss who did not get enough from finasteride. Most providers want to see about a year of consistent finasteride use with progress photos before switching, since some men simply need more time on the first drug. Fast early loss can move the timeline up. It is not used in women who could become pregnant.

    Dosing and Schedule in Los Angeles

    Dutasteride is FDA-approved at 0.5 mg daily for an enlarged prostate and is used off-label at that dose for hair loss. Its long half-life lets some providers use fewer doses per week. Minoxidil is applied daily or taken orally. Low-dose oral minoxidil is an off-label alternative.

    What to Expect Month by Month

    Shedding can pick up in months one and two, usually from minoxidil. The early shed is normal and usually fades. By months three to six, many men notice less loss and some thickening. Comparison trials favored dutasteride over finasteride for hair counts. Give it a year.

    Side Effects of Dutasteride + Minoxidil in Los Angeles

    Dutasteride can lower libido, affect erections or reduce ejaculate volume in a small share of men, similar to finasteride. The long half-life means side effects can take weeks to months to clear. Mood changes should be reported right away. Minoxidil can irritate the scalp. The oral form sometimes causes fluid retention.

    Eligibility and Screening

    Your provider asks about liver health, mood history, sexual health and current medications. Dutasteride lowers PSA by about half, so any doctor screening your prostate needs to know you take it and will usually double the reading to interpret it. No blood donation during use or for six months after. Women who might be pregnant should not handle them.

    How It Compares to Other Options in Los Angeles

    Finasteride + minoxidil is the usual first step, and both parts have FDA approval for hair loss. Dutasteride + minoxidil is stronger but off-label for hair in the US, and its long half-life makes it slower to wash out if side effects show up. Minoxidil + GHK-Cu is a topical-only choice. Transplants help some men.
