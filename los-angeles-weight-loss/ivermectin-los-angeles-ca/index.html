    How Ivermectin Works in Los Angeles

    Ivermectin binds to channels in the nerve and muscle cells of certain parasites. The parasite becomes paralyzed and dies. People tolerate standard doses well, largely because the blood-brain barrier keeps most of the drug away from human nerve cells, which is why overdoses become dangerous. Oral ivermectin tablets are FDA-approved for intestinal strongyloidiasis and onchocerciasis. Clinicians also use it for scabies and lice in appropriate cases.

    Who Ivermectin Is For

    Adults with a confirmed or strongly suspected strongyloides infection are the classic candidates. Strongyloides usually enters through skin on contaminated soil in tropical regions and can persist silently for many years. It is also used for scabies. Oral ivermectin is most useful for crusted scabies or when topical permethrin did not clear it. It has no role in weight loss.

    Ivermectin Dosing and Schedule in Los Angeles

    Ivermectin is dosed by body weight, commonly around 200 micrograms per kilogram as a single oral dose. An adult weighing about 180 pounds typically takes five or six 3 mg tablets, depending on rounding. Take it on an empty stomach with water. Scabies plans commonly use a second dose a week or two after the first, while strongyloides may need one or two days in a row. Your label lists the exact tablet count and dates.

    What to Expect After Treatment

    Active treatment is one or two doses, so the hard part is usually waiting. A day of mild dizziness or nausea is not unusual. Scabies itch can last two to four weeks after the mites are gone, because the skin keeps reacting while it heals, and that alone is not treatment failure. Hot-wash your bedding. Expect a check-in around week four.

    Ivermectin Side Effects in Los Angeles

    Dizziness, nausea, diarrhea and itching are the common side effects. A reaction to dying parasites can cause a temporary rash, fever or swelling in some infections. Serious effects are uncommon. Confusion, unsteady walking and severe rashes are rare at correct doses, and most cases reported to poison centers involve animal products or self-dosing. Seek urgent care for any confusion or trouble walking.

    Eligibility and Screening

    Your intake asks about symptoms, travel history, body weight, pregnancy and breastfeeding. Medications come next. Warfarin is a known interaction, and some drugs that affect brain chemistry or liver enzymes need a closer look before ivermectin is prescribed. People with serious liver disease or some neurological disorders may need another option. Suspected strongyloides often needs an antibody test or stool test first.

    How Ivermectin Compares to Other Anti-Parasitics in Los Angeles

    Mebendazole and albendazole are usually preferred for pinworm, roundworm, whipworm and hookworm. Ivermectin is the first choice for strongyloides. Permethrin cream remains the usual starting point for scabies, and oral ivermectin steps in when the case is severe, widespread at home or did not respond to the cream. If you are not sure what you have, the antiparasitic protocol starts with that question. Your provider picks the drug with the best evidence.
