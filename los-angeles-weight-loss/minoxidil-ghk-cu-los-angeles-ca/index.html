    How Minoxidil + GHK-Cu Works on Thinning Hair in Los Angeles, CA

    How Minoxidil + GHK-Cu Works in Los Angeles

    Minoxidil keeps follicles in their growth phase longer and nudges shrunken follicles to produce thicker strands, probably by improving blood flow around the root. GHK-Cu, a small copper-binding peptide found naturally in blood, has mostly been studied in skin, where it appears to help with repair. Two ingredients, one bottle. Minoxidil alone is FDA-approved as an over-the-counter topical for hereditary hair loss at 2% and 5% strengths. The compounded combination is not FDA-approved, and the hair evidence for GHK-Cu is early.

    Who Minoxidil + GHK-Cu Is For

    Pattern hair loss is the target. Men tend to notice it at the temples or crown, while women more often see the part getting wider over a year or two. It suits people who prefer a non-hormonal route or who already tried plain minoxidil and want a single daily serum with an added peptide. Round bald patches, scarring conditions and sudden shedding after surgery or illness need a different workup.

    Dosing and Daily Routine in Los Angeles

    About one milliliter goes on a dry scalp, once or twice a day depending on your prescription. Part the hair over the thin areas, apply with the dropper and spread it with your fingertips, then wash your hands. Let it dry before bed. More serum is not better, since extra just runs onto the forehead and irritates skin. Your provider chooses the minoxidil strength, often 5% for men and 2% to 5% for women, based on your pattern and how sensitive your skin is.

    What to Expect Month by Month

    Expect more shedding first. Between weeks two and eight, resting hairs often fall out as new growth pushes through, which is unsettling but usually a sign the medication is active. By month three or four, shedding usually drops and fine new hairs appear along the part or crown. Real density changes take six to twelve months of daily use. Take photos in the same light every month, because day-to-day mirror checks are not reliable.

    Side Effects of Minoxidil + GHK-Cu in Los Angeles

    Scalp itching and flaking are the most common complaints, and they often come from the alcohol or propylene glycol base rather than the minoxidil itself. Some people grow fine facial hair when the serum runs onto the skin, which fades after stopping. Early shedding happens too. Report lightheadedness, a racing pulse or puffy ankles right away, since those are rare signs the medication is reaching the rest of the body. GHK-Cu is usually well tolerated, though copper peptides can sting on broken skin.

    Eligibility and Screening

    Your provider asks when the thinning started, whether a parent had it, and whether anything changed in the three months before it began. Recent childbirth, crash dieting, a new medication or a serious illness can all trigger shedding that looks like pattern loss. They also screen for heart disease, low blood pressure, pregnancy, breastfeeding and allergies. Sunburned or infected scalps wait. If your answers hint at thyroid disease or low iron, you may be asked to get a ferritin and TSH test first.

    How It Compares to Finasteride + Minoxidil in Los Angeles

    Finasteride and dutasteride lower DHT, the hormone most tied to male pattern hair loss, so they address the cause more directly than a topical can. Finasteride + Minoxidil and Dutasteride + Minoxidil are usually stronger options for men with a clear male pattern. This serum is non-hormonal. Women who might become pregnant and men worried about libido often choose it for that reason. Some patients start here and add an oral option at the six month check-in if progress is slow.
